For stiff or achy wrists and forearms after typing, phone use or gripping, or as a warm-up before
pushing and pressing. You can do it all at a desk.

Move slowly and stay in a comfortable range. A stretch should feel mild: tightness or a gentle pull
in the forearm is normal, pain is not. Skip any step that hurts.

Stop, and see a doctor or physiotherapist, if you have:

- sharp or severe pain;
- numbness, tingling or pins and needles in the hand or fingers;
- swelling, or a wrist that has changed shape or colour;
- pain after a fall or other injury;
- pain that isn't better after about two weeks, gets worse or keeps coming back.

This session is for everyday stiffness. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything.
