## Today

Today shows the day's workout. On an empty day the card at the top offers the routine your active
program plans for that weekday, with **Start workout**. Other routines are listed below it; one tap
applies one. **Add exercise** starts a day without a routine.

Use the arrows or the week strip to look at another day. Other days offer **Use routine**, which
loads the plan without starting it. The calendar button opens the month.

**Workout options** holds the rest: add an exercise, choose a routine, copy a previous day, session
details, reorder, finish or reopen, and delete the day. Deleting offers **Undo**.

## During a workout

The workout screen shows one exercise at a time as a ring of its sets. Set the load and reps with
the steppers, then **Complete set**. The rest countdown starts on the same ring.

- **+15 s** adds rest and **Skip rest** ends it. A rest that runs out beeps and keeps counting until you
  go on.
- A timed set uses **Start timer**. Pause, resume or stop it; at zero the set is saved.
- The arrows move to the previous and next exercise. Rest keeps running when you move.

Pull up the sheet at the bottom for **All sets**. There you can edit a set, tick or untick it, add or
swipe away sets, add warm-up sets, and see the plates for a barbell. Tap a set's number to give it an
RPE or mark it as a bodyweight set.

**Finish workout** is in the menu at the top. The summary shows the time, sets, volume and any new
records, with room for notes and how tired you felt.

A warm-up section runs one set at a time on its own screen, then goes on to the first exercise.

Rest and timers keep going with the screen off or after leaving the app. A notification shows the
countdown.

## Routines and exercises

**Train** opens on your routines. Tap one to edit it; **Use today** there loads it into today.

In a routine, **Add exercise** opens the library. Tap an exercise to add it with its usual sets,
reps and rest. To make a new one, search for its name and choose **Create & add**.

Tap a row to change its sets, reps or time, rest and section (Warm-up, Main, Stretch, or your own).
**More** holds ranges, set time, record as and notes, plus progression and warm-up sets for an
exercise with a weight. Changing a routine never changes another routine or a workout already
logged.

Drag the handles to reorder. Swipe a row away to remove it; **Undo** brings it back. The Undo bar
can be swiped away.

**Exercises** lists the library by category, with search. An exercise page shows its details, your
history with it and a progress chart. Archive an exercise to hide it without losing its history.

## Programs

A program gives each weekday a routine or a rest. One program is active at a time. Its routine for
the day is what Today offers.

Set **Week 1 starts** to count weeks. With **Deload every** set, each deload week cuts the loads by
the program's percent, and Today says **Deload week**.

## Progression

For an exercise with a weight, **Add weight** in a routine row's **More** raises the load next time
when every set reached the top of its rep range. With it on, **If RPE at most** also asks that the
sets felt easy enough. Deload days don't count.

## Recover

**Recover** lists short sessions by body area. Tap **Start** on a session to run it: one step at a
time, with a pause before each to get into position. Steps done on each side run twice.

Open a session to read its steps and sources, pin it, or delete it. Pinned sessions come first and
also appear when you long-press the app's icon.

A session can have a **Daily reminder** at a time you pick. Tapping the reminder starts the session.
There is none on a day you already did it.

## Lessons

**Train → Lessons** holds notes for teaching yourself an exercise. **Practice** runs the lesson's
drill like a recovery session, and the lesson counts how often you practised. An exercise's page
links to its lessons.

## History and body weight

**More → History** shows the month with a dot for each workout and a dash for each recovery session.
Tap a day to see it under the calendar; **Open day** shows it on Today.

**More → Body weight** keeps your readings and their trend.

## Your own content

Routines, exercises, programs, recovery sessions and lessons can be written as text files and
imported. **More → Import content** takes one .md file or a .zip pack with images, shows what is
new and what it would replace, and imports all of it or nothing. You can also share a file to Fit
from another app.

The format is described at
[the content format](https://github.com/MoXcz/fit/blob/main/docs/content-format.md).

## Backup

**More → Backup and data** exports everything as one .zip file and restores it. Restoring replaces
everything in the app. **Wipe history** clears what you logged and keeps your routines and content.
**Wipe everything** returns the app to a fresh install. Export a backup first: a wipe can't be
undone.

## Settings

- **Alarm**: the beep, vibration and notification when a rest or timed set ends.
- **Keep screen on** while a workout or session is open.
- **Auto-advance**: timed steps in a session move on by themselves.
- **Unit** for exercises you make in the app, and **Bar weight** for the plate calculator.

Nothing in Fit goes to the internet. Links open in your browser.
